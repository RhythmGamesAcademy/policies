\chapter{Amendment of the Regulations}
\begin{articles}
  \item This chapter establishes the necessary provisions concerning procedures for adopting, amending, and repealing these Regulations, in accordance with the principles of the Charter.
  \item A Dean may propose the adoption, amendment, or repeal of these Regulations.
  \item When making a proposal under the preceding Article, the Dean must seek Students' views as necessary.
  \item The adoption, amendment, or repeal of these Regulations takes effect when the proposer has notified the other Deans in advance.
  \item When these Regulations are adopted, amended, or repealed, the Director must promptly announce this to Students.
  \item The Deans separately establish provisions needed to implement this chapter.
\end{articles}

\bigskip
\noindent Supplementary provisions
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.,labelsep=.5em,leftmargin=*]
  \item These Regulations take effect on 1 September 2026.
  \item Notwithstanding Chapter 2, Article 3, people already engaged in the Academy's administration when these Regulations take effect are deemed to have been appointed as Deans, unless they do not wish to be appointed.
\end{enumerate}
