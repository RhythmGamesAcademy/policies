\chapter{General Provisions}
\begin{articles}
  \item These Regulations establish the necessary provisions concerning the organization and operation of Rhythm Games Academy (hereinafter referred to as the ``Academy'') in accordance with the principles of the Academy Charter (hereinafter referred to as the ``Charter'').
  \item In these Regulations, the following terms have the meanings specified below.
  \begin{subitems}
    \item Student: a person who has acquired Student status under Chapter 4, Article 3.
    \item Dean: a Student appointed under Chapter 2, Article 3.
    \item Director: the Dean designated as Director under Chapter 2, Article 4.
    \item Lecturer: a Student approved under Chapter 6, Article 2.
    \item officeholder: the Director, a Dean, a Lecturer, or a person holding another office established within the Academy under Chapter 2, Article 2.
    \item course: a learning activity provided by a Lecturer to Students.
    \item academic term: a unit used to divide the Academy's activities, consisting of a six-month period specified in Chapter 3, Article 2.
    \item regular activity period: a period during which the Academy's courses and other activities are conducted in their usual form.
    \item make-up period: a period following the regular activity period for supplementary course sessions within the same academic term.
    \item recruitment period: a period during which people wishing to become Students are recruited for the next academic term.
  \end{subitems}
  \item The members referred to in the Charter are Students.
  \item The Management referred to in the Charter consists of the Deans.
  \item Guidelines may be established to provide for matters necessary to implement these Regulations and otherwise operate the Academy.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item Guidelines may not contain provisions contrary to the Charter or these Regulations. Guidelines that conflict with the Charter or these Regulations have no effect.
    \item Guidelines may not impose obligations on Students that are not provided for in these Regulations.
    \item The Deans adopt, amend, and repeal guidelines.
    \item When guidelines concerning Students' activities are adopted, amended, or repealed, the Deans must promptly announce this to Students.
  \end{paragraphs}
\end{articles}
