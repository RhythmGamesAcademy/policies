\chapter{Students}
\begin{articles}
  \item This chapter establishes the necessary provisions concerning Student status, joining and leaving the Academy, and other matters relating to membership, in accordance with the principles of the Charter.
  \item A person who can comply with these Regulations may become a Student.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item Notwithstanding the preceding paragraph, a person who does not meet the terms of use of an external service used as a platform for the Academy's activities may not become a Student, because they cannot participate in activities on that service.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item A person wishing to become a Student must submit the prescribed application and acquires Student status when that application reaches the Academy while it is accepting new applications. No approval or selection is required for the application.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item Where operational needs require it, the Deans may set a limit on the number of people who newly acquire Student status during each recruitment period specified in Chapter 3, Article 3.
    \item When the limit in the preceding paragraph is reached, the Deans must close acceptance of further new applications and promptly announce the time of closure.
    \item The limit described in the preceding two paragraphs applies only to people who newly become Students during the recruitment period concerned. It does not limit existing Students' continued membership or the total number of Students.
    \item The specific application procedure referred to in paragraph 1 is established separately.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item Students may freely participate in inquiry activities within the Academy as provided in the Charter.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item Students must comply with these Regulations and endeavor to maintain order within the community.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item Once a Student has completed the procedure for joining, their membership continues across academic terms without any procedure to renew it each term.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item The preceding paragraph does not prevent withdrawal under the following Article or expulsion under Article 7, or other grounds for loss of Student status.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item A Student may leave the Academy at any time.
  \item A Student loses Student status when expelled under Chapter 8.
  \item The Deans separately establish provisions needed to implement this chapter.
\end{articles}
