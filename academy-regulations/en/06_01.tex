\chapter{Lecturers}
\begin{articles}
  \item This chapter establishes the necessary provisions concerning Lecturer status and related matters, in accordance with the principles of the Charter.
  \item A Student may become a Lecturer by agreeing to the following matters, submitting the prescribed application, and receiving approval.
  \begin{subitems}
    \item The Academy may use and distribute course materials created by that Student free of charge, to the extent necessary for activities within the Academy.
    \item The Student will comply with the separately established guidelines concerning Lecturers.
  \end{subitems}
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item The approval referred to in the preceding paragraph requires the unanimous agreement of the Deans.
    \item The application procedure referred to in paragraph 1 is established separately.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item Lecturers may determine the content, structure, and conduct of their courses at their own discretion.
  \item Lecturers must endeavor to conduct their courses in good faith.
  \item Matters concerning loss of Lecturer status are governed by Chapter 2, Article 7 and Chapter 8.
  \item The Deans separately establish provisions needed to implement this chapter.
\end{articles}
