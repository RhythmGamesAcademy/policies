\chapter{Deans}
\begin{articles}
  \item This chapter sets out the fundamental principles that Deans must follow in performing their duties, in accordance with the principles of the Charter.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item Specific procedures concerning the duties of Deans are established separately.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item Deans are responsible for the Academy's administrative affairs and have a duty to maintain an environment in which Students can continuously pursue the inquiry activities provided for in the Charter.
  \item Deans must always recognize that their duties exist to enable and support Students' inquiry activities.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item Deans must support Students' inquiry activities equally, regardless of their form or subject, and recognize that they are not in a position to judge the relative merits of those activities.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item The office of Dean is a function assigned for the performance of duties and does not signify personal superiority over Students.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item Deans perform their duties while retaining Student status and must engage with other Students as equals.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item A Dean's intervention in Students' activities must be limited to the minimum necessary so as not to unduly restrict the academic freedom provided for in the Charter.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item Where several means are available, Deans must give preference to those that are less restrictive and easier to reverse or recover from.
    \item Deans may exercise their powers only where grounds specified in these Regulations exist and must not use those powers for other purposes.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item Deans must remain able to explain the reasons for decisions made in the course of their duties.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item When a Dean makes a decision affecting a Student's rights or interests, the Dean must make the reasons clear to that Student.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item Deans must recognize that their work should be passed on despite changes in membership and endeavor to record the standards and circumstances of their decisions.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item Deans must endeavor to document procedures so that the administrative work for which they are responsible does not depend on a particular individual.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item Where there is more than one Dean, they must share information and cooperate in performing their duties.
  \item The Deans separately establish provisions needed to implement this chapter.
\end{articles}
