\chapter{Management and Organization}
\begin{articles}
  \item This chapter establishes the necessary provisions concerning offices required for the Academy's operation, their appointment, and related matters, in accordance with the principles of the Charter.
  \item The Academy has a Director and Deans.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item In addition to the offices specified in the preceding paragraph, the Academy may establish Lecturer positions and other offices.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item A Student wishing to become a Dean must make a request to the Director.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item When the Director approves the request referred to in the preceding paragraph, the Director appoints that Student as a Dean.
    \item No fixed number of Deans is prescribed.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item One of the Deans serves as Director.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item If the office of Director becomes vacant, the Deans determine a successor through consultation.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item If consultation among the Deans does not produce agreement, or if an urgent decision is required, the Director may decide the matter.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item The preceding paragraph does not apply to the following matters.
    \begin{subitems}
      \item Approval under Chapter 6, Article 2 or Chapter 7, Article 6.
      \item Matters concerning removal of the Director from office.
    \end{subitems}
    \item A decision under paragraph 1 is a procedure for facilitating decision-making among the Deans and does not mean that the Director is superior to other Deans.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item Officeholders serve without remuneration. They must not receive remuneration or other compensation for performing their duties.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item The preceding paragraph does not prevent an officeholder from accepting a customary courtesy gift that does not constitute compensation for performing duties.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item An officeholder who loses Student status automatically loses their status as an officeholder.
  \item Matters concerning loss of officeholder status other than those specified in the preceding Article are governed by Chapter 8.
  \item The Deans separately establish provisions needed to implement this chapter.
\end{articles}
