\chapter{Academic Terms}
\begin{articles}
  \item This chapter establishes the necessary provisions concerning academic terms as units of activity periods for the smooth conduct of the Academy's activities, in accordance with the principles of the Charter.
  \item The Academy's academic terms consist of the following periods, recurring every six months.
  \begin{subitems}
    \item From 1 April to 30 September.
    \item From 1 October to 31 March of the following year.
  \end{subitems}
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item The regular activity period and make-up period for each academic term in the preceding paragraph are as follows.
    \begin{subitems}
      \item For the academic term in item (1) of the preceding paragraph, the regular activity period runs from 1 April to 31 July, and the make-up period from 1 August to 15 August.
      \item For the academic term in item (2) of the preceding paragraph, the regular activity period runs from 1 October to 31 January of the following year, and the make-up period from 1 February to 15 February of the following year.
    \end{subitems}
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item Recruitment periods for the following academic terms are as specified below.
  \begin{subitems}
    \item For the academic term in Article 2, paragraph 1, item (1): from 15 February to 15 April.
    \item For the academic term in Article 2, paragraph 1, item (2): from 15 August to 15 October.
  \end{subitems}
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item A recruitment period may continue after the beginning of the regular activity period of the academic term concerned.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item The first academic term when the Academy begins full course operation is designated ``Term \#1''. Subsequent terms are numbered in order of their start dates, increasing by one each time, regardless of which period in Article 2, paragraph 1 they fall within.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item The Deans separately establish the start date of the first academic term and other necessary matters.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item When the Deans determine that unavoidable operational circumstances exist, they may change the periods specified in Articles 2 and 3.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item When a period is changed under the preceding paragraph, the Deans must promptly announce the change to Students.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item The Deans separately establish provisions needed to implement this chapter.
\end{articles}
