\chapter{Removal from Office and Disciplinary Measures}
\begin{articles}
  \item This chapter establishes the necessary provisions concerning the resignation and removal of officeholders and disciplinary measures against Students, including officeholders, in accordance with the principles of the Charter.
  \item An officeholder other than the Director may resign at any time by notifying the Director.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item The Director may resign at any time by notifying the Deans.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item If the Director determines that an officeholder other than the Director falls within either of the following items, the Director may remove that officeholder from office after consultation among the Deans.
  \begin{subitems}
    \item The officeholder is unable to perform their duties, thereby hindering the smooth operation of the Academy.
    \item Harassment or other seriously improper conduct toward other Students makes the officeholder unfit to be entrusted with the duties of the office.
  \end{subitems}
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item If the Deans determine that the Director falls within either item of the preceding paragraph, they may remove the Director from office through consultation.
    \item Removal under the preceding two paragraphs does not replace disciplinary measures under this chapter. Where the Deans consider it necessary, they may impose disciplinary measures separately under this chapter.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item A Student may be subject to disciplinary measures for conduct falling within any of the following items.
  \begin{subitems}
    \item Conduct in violation of the Charter or these Regulations.
    \item Harassment or other seriously improper conduct toward other Students.
    \item False statements or other fraudulent conduct in applications or notifications.
    \item Conduct that seriously damages the Academy's reputation or credibility.
    \item Conduct in violation of the terms of use of an external service used as a platform for the Academy's activities that hinders those activities.
    \item In addition to the preceding items, conduct that seriously harms other Students' rights or interests.
  \end{subitems}
  \item Where the Deans determine that facts falling within any item of the preceding Article exist, they may impose the following disciplinary measures on the Student concerned.
  \begin{subitems}
    \item Warning.
    \item Suspension of activities: suspension of all or part of the Student's activities within the Academy for a specified period.
    \item Expulsion.
  \end{subitems}
  \item As a general rule, disciplinary measures are imposed progressively, in the order of warning, suspension of activities, and expulsion.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item If the Deans determine that a Student who has received a warning again engages in conduct falling within any item of Article 4 during the same academic term, they may impose suspension of activities.
    \item If the Deans determine that a Student who has received suspension of activities again engages in conduct falling within any item of Article 4 during or after the suspension period, they may impose expulsion.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item Notwithstanding the preceding Article, where the Deans determine that an exceptionally serious violation has occurred, they may immediately impose suspension of activities or expulsion without first issuing a warning.
  \item If the Deans determine that a Student's conduct is causing harm to order within the Academy or to the safety of other Students, or poses an imminent risk of such harm, they may take emergency measures to the extent necessary, such as temporarily restricting that Student's access to activities within the Academy, without imposing disciplinary measures under Articles 5 to 7.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item When a measure under the preceding paragraph is taken, the Deans must notify the Student concerned of the reasons without delay.
    \item A measure under paragraph 1 ceases to have effect no later than the last day of the academic term in which it was taken.
    \item A measure under paragraph 1 does not replace disciplinary measures under this chapter. Where the Deans consider it necessary, they may impose disciplinary measures separately under this chapter.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item A Student may appeal to the Deans against removal from office under Article 3, a measure under the preceding Article, or a disciplinary measure under this chapter.
  \begin{paragraphs}
    \item An appeal under the preceding paragraph is reviewed by Deans other than those who made the removal, emergency measure, or disciplinary decision concerned.
  \end{paragraphs}
  \item The Deans separately establish provisions needed to implement this chapter.
\end{articles}
