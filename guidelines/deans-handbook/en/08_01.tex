\chapter{Activity Environment and Handover of Responsibilities}

\section{Changing Spaces or Permissions}

Before establishing a new space, the person responsible determines and communicates its purpose, intended users, and who will manage it.
When reorganizing an existing space, check Students' usage and activity records that should be retained, and tell affected people what will change and when.
Grant viewing and editing permissions only to the extent needed for the work. Do not treat the technical permissions of an office as a hierarchy among Students.
Do not create prohibitions or grounds for discipline absent from the Regulations merely through a space's instructions.

\section{Checking Settings and Automation}

The person who changes a setting records its purpose, the change, the behavior checked, and how to reverse it.
Repetitive administrative work may be automated, but do not finalize conclusions affecting Student status, approval, discipline, or other rights or interests solely from automated processing.
The Dean responsible must know the contact point and correction procedure for unintended results.

\section{Handover of Responsibilities}

The outgoing person tells their successor about ongoing applications and inquiries, reasons for decisions, announced guidance, space settings, and the location of records.
The successor checks whether the transferred permissions allow them to perform the necessary work and removes the predecessor's permissions that are no longer needed.
Hand over course registration forms and response sheets using the procedures in Chapter 4 of this Handbook.
Do not treat undecided matters discovered during handover as institutional decisions solely because they were the predecessor's customary practice.
