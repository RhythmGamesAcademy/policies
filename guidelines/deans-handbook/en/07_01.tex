\chapter{Official Materials, Amendments, and Announcements}

\section{Preparing Guidance}

The person responsible checks the current Academy Charter, Academy Regulations, and announced guidance for the systems and practices described in the material.
Do not describe numbers under consideration or systems planned for the next academic term as already decided or implemented.
State the material's intended audience, update date, and references. Before publication, check provision references, the presence of personal information, and wording with another Dean.
This check is to confirm accuracy; it does not add an approval procedure absent from the Regulations.

\section{Amending Regulations or Guidelines}

Follow Chapter 9 of the Academy Regulations when amending the Regulations.
Distinguish the proposal by a Dean, its taking effect through advance notification to the other Deans, and the Director's prompt announcement to Students.
Seek Students' views as necessary.
Under Chapter 1, Article 5 of the Regulations, guidelines must comply with the Charter and Regulations and must not create Student obligations absent from the Regulations.
Promptly announce the adoption, amendment, or repeal of guidelines concerning Students' activities.
Do not treat editing source files or sharing a draft as completion of these procedures or announcements.

\section{Checks and Records after a Change}

The person responsible records the changes, grounds, timing of advance notifications and announcements, and related guidance updated.
When changing guidance, check application forms, the official website, pinned posts within the Academy, and other relevant locations for outdated explanations, and pass necessary corrections to the people responsible.
Make clear the reasons for changes and communications to affected Students, and continue to identify undecided matters as undecided.
