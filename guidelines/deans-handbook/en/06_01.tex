\chapter{Inquiries and Consultations}

\section{Receiving an Inquiry}

When receiving an inquiry through the Academy's communication channels or an announced external contact point, the person responsible checks what the person wants to know, when a response is needed, and whether any circumstances concern Student safety.
Respond to inquiries from people who are not Students within the scope of information that can be disclosed.
For a consultation, check whether the person only wants an answer or is asking for action or to report a problem.
If imminent harm is suspected, consider the emergency measures in Chapter 5 of this Handbook.

\section{Responses and Handover}

When explaining an established system, point to the relevant Regulations or announced guidance.
For undecided matters, such as numbers, application dates, or the Academy's formal grades and credits system, say that they are undecided. Do not promise the outcome of an application or disciplinary decision in advance.
If you cannot decide alone, explain that you will check and follow up. Pass the main points of the inquiry and what you have already said to the other Deans.
For answers interpreting the system or setting a precedent, record the grounds, the person who responded, and the time of the communication.

\section{Questions about Copyrighted Works}

Discussing a work, posting audio or images, using such material in course materials, and publishing course materials outside the Academy involve different actions and different scopes of disclosure.
Confirm which action the person is asking about and advise them to check the material's rights holder, terms of use, permission, or applicable legal basis.
Do not promise that citing a source permits every use of the material.
For use within the Academy of course materials created by a Lecturer, check the scope of Chapter 6, Article 2 and Chapter 7, Article 8 of the Academy Regulations. Do not assume that permission to publish outside the Academy has been granted.
If judgment is difficult, avoid a definitive answer, record what needs to be checked, and share it among the Deans.
