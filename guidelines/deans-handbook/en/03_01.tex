\chapter{Applications to Become a Lecturer or Open a Course}

\section{Receiving an Application to Become a Lecturer}

Check for a basis in knowledge or experience that the applicant can share with other Students in their proposed field, but do not make competitive results, years of experience, or completion of the achievements field a universal requirement.
If the proposed field is narrow, you may suggest broadening it as an option, but do not make this a condition of approval.

\section{Receiving an Application to Open a Course}

The person responsible checks that the applicant is a Lecturer, that the required application information is present, that course information is provided, and whether there are any conditions for taking the course.
As a general rule, handle complete applications with a view to approval so that Lecturers can carry out their activities.
Under Chapter 7, Article 5 of the Academy Regulations, Faculties and course categories are guides for finding courses.
For a course spanning several categories, selecting the principal category is sufficient; suggest a different category as advice.
Do not refuse approval solely because a topic is unusual, narrow, or overlaps an existing course.
The number of sessions is the Lecturer's plan, and the content, structure, and conduct of the course are at the Lecturer's discretion (Chapter 6, Article 3 of the Regulations).
Explain the make-up period provisions in Chapter 7, Article 9 of the Regulations for sessions that are difficult to conduct as planned.

\section{Corrections, Approval, and Notification of the Result}

If information is missing or facts are uncertain, the person responsible checks with the applicant and explains specifically what can be corrected.
The grounds for asking a Lecturer to discontinue or change a course under Chapter 7, Article 10 of the Academy Regulations do not directly establish criteria for approving course applications. Do not mechanically use those grounds alone to refuse an application.
Share the corrected materials and the grounds for the decision with the Deans. Approval of Lecturer applications and course applications both requires unanimous agreement of the Deans, under Chapter 6, Article 2, paragraph 2 and Chapter 7, Article 6, paragraph 2 of the Regulations, respectively.
Do not substitute a decision by the Director alone for that approval.

Notify the applicant of the result. If approval is refused, state the established facts and reasons (Chapter 5, Article 6, paragraph 2 of the Regulations).
The person responsible records the application, requests for corrections, each Dean's judgment, the conclusion, and communications.
If the applicant asks for the reasons to be checked again or for reconsideration, discuss the request among the Deans.
