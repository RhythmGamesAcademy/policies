\chapter{Joining the Academy and Continuing Membership}

\section{Starting Recruitment}

Deans check the recruitment periods in Chapter 3, Article 3 and the provisions of Chapter 4, Article 3 of the Academy Regulations, and announce how applications are accepted.
If operational needs require a limit on the number of new Students, announce the limit and application method before accepting applications.
The limit applies to new admissions during that recruitment period; it is not a limit on total membership or the duration of existing Students' membership.

\section{When the Limit Is Reached}

When the limit is reached, close acceptance of new applications and promptly announce the closure and its timing.
For inquiries after closure, explain that applications are not currently being accepted and provide only information already decided about the next recruitment period.

\section{Withdrawal and Loss of Status}

Students continue their membership without applying again each academic term and may leave at any time (Chapter 4, Articles 5 and 6 of the Regulations).
On receiving a request to withdraw, the person responsible confirms the person's identity and the relevant records, and records how the withdrawal is handled and any necessary communications.
Loss of status through expulsion follows the disciplinary procedures in Chapter 8 of the Regulations. Do not deprive an existing Student of their status because of admission numbers or how often they participate.
